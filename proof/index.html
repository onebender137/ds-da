FAQ

Everything you'd want to know, in plain language. The short version: your proof lives on the blockchain, not on us.

What is this, exactly?

An immutable, BSV-anchored proof that you created something, at a specific time, and it has not changed since. We take your file, fingerprint it (SHA-256 hash), + anchor that fingerprint on the BSV blockchain. The blockchain is the timestamp; the hash is the proof.

Think of it like a notary public, but the notary is a blockchain that never sleeps, never loses the record, + anyone in the world can check the stamp.

How does it actually work?

Basic tier: We hash your file (SHA-256 — a 64-character fingerprint) + put that hash on-chain via an OP_RETURN transaction. The file stays with you; the chain proves it existed at time T + is unchanged.

Premium tier: We put the entire file on-chain (BSV's unbounded blocks allow MB-scale data for pennies). You can rebuild the file from the chain alone — no server, no us, just the block.

What if your site goes down?

Your proof is not on our site. It's on the BSV blockchain — a decentralized network of thousands of nodes. Our website is just a reader for the chain. If we vanish tomorrow, anyone with a txid can still pull up the certificate + verify it, using any BSV explorer + the blockchain itself. The record outlives us.

What if I lose my file?

Basic tier: The hash proves the file existed + is unchanged, but you need a copy to verify against the hash. (Like a certificate of authenticity — it proves the painting is real, but it's not the painting.)

Premium tier: The full file is on-chain. Lose your copy? Rebuild it from the blockchain. We've recovered a 136 KB photo + an 8 MB GIF straight off the chain. The chain is the backup.

How do I verify a proof (mine or someone else's)?

Three ways, all trustless:

1. Our lookup form — drop the txid in, + we pull up the certificate + the verify tool.

2. Any BSV explorer — go to whatsonchain.com/tx/<txid> + see the OP_RETURN data.

3. Re-hash — take the file, SHA-256 it, + compare to the on-chain hash. Match = unchanged.

No login, no account, no trust in us. The math is the trust.

What does it cost?

The BSV data fee is ~0.1 sat/byte (calibrated to real transactions). A 10 MB song is ~20 cents on-chain; a 50 MB video ~$1. We charge a flat service fee (well above the on-chain cost) — the blockchain is essentially subsidizing your storage.

You pay in BSV to our notary address, + we verify the payment on-chain before anchoring. No payment, no anchor.

Why BSV, specifically?

Three reasons: (1) Unbounded blocks — BSV allows MB-scale data in a single transaction (Bitcoin caps at 1 MB; BSV goes to 2 GB blocks). (2) Cheap data — the data fee is ~0.1 sat/byte, so a full song is pennies. (3) Permanent — the BSV blockchain is decentralized + has been running since 2018. It's not going away.

What can I anchor?

Anything with bytes: photos, MP3s, videos, documents, contracts, designs, code, patents, tweets, art, recipes, love letters. If it's a file, it can be anchored. We've tested photos, MP3s, GIFs, + text — all work.

Is it really permanent?

As permanent as the BSV blockchain — which is as permanent as a network of thousands of independent nodes all agreeing on the same record. No single point of failure. No server to crash. No company to go bankrupt. The proof will outlive us, your hard drive, + probably your grandchildren's smartphones.

Do I need to trust you?

No. That's the whole point. The proof is verifiable by anyone, with just the txid + the BSV blockchain. You don't need our website, our servers, or our word. The blockchain is the notary; the hash is the stamp; the math is the trust.

We're the convenience layer — the pretty interface, the verify tool, the share button. But the record is yours, on the chain, forever.
