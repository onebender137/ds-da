How to play Links

1

Aim

Drag anywhere to aim — the dotted arc and gold ring show your predicted carry with the current wind. Tap ◎ LANDING to fly the camera out to where it'll come down, and keep aiming from there. Pinch or scroll to zoom. The compass shows where the wind pushes the ball.

DRAGA / D fine aimWHEEL zoomV flyoverT landing view
2

Three-tap swing

Tap SWING to start the gauge. Tap again to lock power. A marker slides down — tap when it crosses the gold band for a pure strike. Early = pull-hook left, late = push-slice right.

SPACE ×3← → aim↑ ↓ clubESC pauseM muteTAB card
3

Read the ground

On the green the dotted line previews your putt's break — aim off-line and let the slope feed it in. Water and out-of-bounds cost +1 and a re-drop. Rounds post Stableford points to the arcade board: par 2 · birdie 3 · eagle 4 · bogey 1.
