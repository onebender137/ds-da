How to play Links

1

Aim

Drag anywhere to aim — the dotted arc and gold ring show your predicted carry with the current wind. Pinch or scroll to zoom. The compass shows where the wind pushes the ball.

DRAGA / D fine aimWHEEL zoomV flyover
2

Three-tap swing

Tap SWING to start the gauge. Tap again to lock power. A marker slides down — tap in the gold zone for a pure strike. Early = slice right, late = hook left.

SPACE ×3Q / E clubESC pauseM muteTAB card
3

Read the ground

On the green the dotted line previews your putt's break — aim off-line and let the slope feed it in. Water and out-of-bounds cost +1 and a re-drop. Rounds post Stableford points to the arcade board: par 2 · birdie 3 · eagle 4 · bogey 1.
