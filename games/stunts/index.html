GAME

Three runs per game. Your score is the tally of all 3 runs — land clean to bank points, wipe out and that run scores zero. Beat your best and post it to the global board.

RIDE

Each run auto-starts after a 3..2..1 countdown — just get ready. You build speed for free. Hold SPACE (or touch-and-hold) on the ramp to charge the power gauge, then release right on the gold line (7/8) as you hit the lip — hit it for max air, crank past it and you lose height.
