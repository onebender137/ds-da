📖 HOW TO BEAVER

THE GOAL

  • Two teams take turns. On your turn, one beaver may move, aim and fire one weapon. Last team standing wins the river.
  • Beavers at 0 HP explode (chain reactions!). Beavers swept into the river are gone for good.
  • After a shot resolves you get a short retreat — walk or hop to safety before the enemy answers.

CONTROLS

←→ Walk (also during retreat) — AD too
↑↓ Aim angle — or just move the mouse
SPACE Hold to charge power, release to fire
ENTER Hop forward
⌫ Backflip (dodge incoming!)
1…0 Quick-select weapons
TAB Open the full arsenal
WHEEL Zoom the battlefield
ESC Pause / cancel targeting
👆 Touch: drag field to aim, hold FIRE to charge
M Mute sound effects — music lives on the DSDA Radio

THE ARSENAL

LODGE WISDOM

  • Wind bends rockets, grenades, mortar shells and bombs — check the gauge every turn. Acorns don't care about wind… much.
  • Tail Slap deals tiny damage but huge knockback. Slap a wounded beaver into the river for an instant KO.
  • Dam Buster hits for 75. Drop it and run — you can walk and hop while the fuse burns. Devious AI does exactly that.
  • Crates parachute in: green crosses heal +30, amber crates restock a random weapon. Walk over them to collect.
  • Falls from high up hurt. Explosions fling beavers — landing badly finishes the job.
  • Round 11+: SUDDEN DEATH. The dam breaks and the river rises every turn. Campers drown.
