HOW TO HUSTLE

bar rules · winner takes all
🎯

Aim & strike

Move the mouse to aim — in the CUE camera it steers by feel, in TOP/TV it points where you look. Click once to lock the aim so you can reach the spin pad or power bar without knocking it off line. Slow mouse moves aim finer than fast ones. Arrow keys nudge (tap) or sweep (hold); hold SHIFT with the mouse or arrows for hair-thin in any camera. Press and pull back to load the cue, release to strike — drag back to zero to cancel. Or hold SPACE, or set the power bar and hit SHOOT. On touch: tap the cloth to point the cue there (TOP/TV), drag anywhere to fine-tune the line (slow drags are finer). To shoot, put your finger on the cue ball and pull straight back, then let go; pull back to zero or slide off to the side to cancel. Pinch to zoom never touches your shot. Or set the power bar and hit SHOOT.

🌀

English

Drag the red dot on the cue-ball widget. Top = follow through the object ball, bottom = draw back, sides = kick off cushions at bent angles. It resets after every strike.

🎱

Bar-rules 8-ball

Break, then the table is open until someone legally pots — that ball picks your group (solids 1–7 or stripes 9–15). Clear your seven, then sink the 8 to win. Pot the 8 early, or scratch while potting it, and you lose on the spot.

⚠️

Fouls & the clock

Scratch, wrong ball first, no contact, or no rail after contact = foul: The Hustler gets ball in hand (tap the cloth to place yours). You have 35 seconds a shot. An 8 potted on the break is re-spotted.

💰

Hustle score

Pot +100 · combo pot +150 · bank shot +100 · the 8 +300 · rack win +750 · break & run +500. Three straight pots light a ×2 heater, six goes ×3. Fouls cost 100. Post your best to the Hall.

🎥

Cameras

CUE rides behind your shot · TOP reads the whole layout · TV broadcasts the action · ORBIT lets you drag around the room. Wheel / pinch zooms every angle.
