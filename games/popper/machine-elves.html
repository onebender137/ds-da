            INSTRUCTIONS

            HOW TO PLAY

            Charge a shot and fire it into the fairyland lattice. Link 3+ same-color blossoms to pop them. Popped blossoms above drop down — chain the drops for bigger combos.

            CONTROLS

            AIM — ← → keys, or swipe to steer the wand.
            CHARGE — hold SPACE (or hold touch) to power the shot, release to fire.

            COMBOS

            4+ SPARK · 5+ MASTER · 10+ PHENOMENAL.
            DOUBLE PUNCH — when a big pop drops a cascade of blossoms (5+), you score a bonus and hear the callout: 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 ball.

            DIFFICULTY — INITIATE vs MASTER

            Pick your path in the menu (it's saved, and each has its own leaderboard).

            INITIATE — the assisted path.
            · Prediction line ON: a guide line shows exactly where your shot will land.
            · Bank shots allowed: your ball can bounce off the left/right walls.
            The Tinkerbell fairy still scouts the landing spot.

            MASTER — the purist path.
            · Prediction line OFF: no guide line — only the fairy marks the landing spot.
            · No bank shots: if your ball hits a wall it's lost (no bounce) — you have to aim it straight into the lattice.
            Harder, and the scores are kept on a separate Masters board.

            TOP ELVES

            Two boards, kept apart: INITIATES (prediction line on) and MASTERS (no line, no bank shots). You rank on the one you played.
            Within a board — #1 Most Phenomenal Elve · #2–5 Apprentice Elves · #6+ rank and file.

            DSDA RADIO

            The top-left radio streams the DSDA network. Tap the pill to open it, pick a station, and use the slider for volume. It auto-folds after 2s.
