What it does

Send & receive

Pay anyone with BSV and get paid in seconds. A ding when money lands, so you know it arrived.

Built-in browser

Open the DSDA Arcade or Proof of Creation right inside the wallet and sign in with one tap. You decide what each site is allowed to do, including how much it can spend without asking.

Your keys stay yours

Keys are made and kept on your phone, protected by Android’s Keystore. We never hold your funds and never see your recovery phrase.

How to check

On your Android phone (easiest)

Install AppVerifier (open source), then install Pocket. Tap Copy for AppVerifier above and paste or share that text into AppVerifier. It should show Pocket as verified. If it doesn’t, uninstall Pocket and don’t use it.

On a computer, before installing

Download the file, then run the command for your system and compare the result to the SHA-256 above. Every character must match. 
Linux:    sha256sum pocket-wallet.apk
Mac:      shasum -a 256 pocket-wallet.apk
Windows:  certutil -hashfile pocket-wallet.apk SHA256

Updates

Android itself refuses to install an update signed with a different certificate. As long as you started from a verified copy, every update after it has to come from us.

Install in a minute

Pocket isn’t on the Play Store yet, so Android installs it straight from the file.

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.The file comes from our public releases page on GitHub.
  2. Open the downloaded file.Android will ask to allow your browser to install apps. Allow it for this install. You can switch it back off afterwards.
  3. Tap Install.Android may warn that the app isn’t from the Play Store. That’s expected for direct downloads, and it’s exactly why the checks above exist.
  4. Open Pocket and create your wallet.Write your recovery phrase on paper and keep it somewhere safe. It’s the only way back into your wallet if you lose your phone.

Stay safe

  • Only download Pocket from this page. We will never send you the app by DM, email, or a link in a comment.
  • No one from DSDA will ever ask for your recovery phrase. Anyone who does is trying to steal from you.
  • Your phrase goes on paper, not in a screenshot or a notes app.
