Fundy Wallet Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Fundy Wallet (“Fundy”) is an Android app made by Dream Syndicate Digital Assets (“DSDA”, “we”), a sole proprietorship in New Brunswick, Canada. This policy explains what Fundy keeps on your phone, what it has to send over the internet to work, and who can see that information.

The short version

  • No accounts. You don’t sign up with us, and we don’t ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking inside the app.
  • Your keys and recovery phrase never leave your phone. We can’t see them, and we can’t recover them for you.
  • We don’t run any server that receives your wallet data. To use the BSV blockchain, Fundy talks to public blockchain services run by other companies, listed below.
  • Blockchain transactions are public and permanent. That’s how the network works, for every wallet.

What stays on your phone

  • Your recovery phrase and private keys, encrypted and protected by Android’s Keystore and, where your phone supports it, your fingerprint or face unlock.
  • Your wallet database: transaction history, contacts, and the records the wallet needs to find and spend your coins.
  • Browser data: bookmarks, history, open tabs and the permissions you’ve given to websites.
  • Your settings.

Fundy opts out of Android’s cloud backup and device-transfer features, and it does not use any online wallet backup service. If you lose your phone, your recovery phrase (or your printed recovery shares) is the only way back into your wallet. Keep it safe and offline.

What leaves your phone, and why

A wallet has to talk to the blockchain to show your balance, find payments sent to you, and send money. When it does, the services involved can see your device’s IP address and the addresses or transactions being looked up. Fundy uses these kinds of services:

  • Finding your balance and payments: Fundy looks up your receiving addresses and their transactions with blockchain data services, including WhatsOnChain and services run by the BSV Association.
  • Sending payments: transactions you send are submitted to the network through transaction processors, such as those run by the BSV Association, TAAL and GorillaPool.
  • Checking payments are real: Fundy downloads block headers from a BSV Association service to verify incoming transactions.
  • Person-to-person payments: when you pay or get paid by another wallet user directly, the payment details travel as encrypted messages through the BSV Association’s MessageBox service, addressed to the wallets’ identity keys.
  • NFTs: to show your NFTs, Fundy looks up your addresses with the 1Sat Ordinals service (api.1sat.app) and loads the NFT content from it.
  • Currency display: Fundy fetches exchange rates so it can show your balance in your chosen currency.

These services are run by third parties under their own privacy policies. We don’t control them and we don’t receive the data you send them.

The built-in browser

Websites you open in Fundy’s browser receive what any browser shares: your IP address, and any cookies or local storage the site sets. A website can ask Fundy for wallet actions, such as your identity key, a signature or a payment. Fundy asks your permission first. Small payments under the limit you set in settings can go through without a prompt; you can lower that limit to zero.

The ds-da.ca website itself uses Google Analytics to count visits. That applies to the website in any browser, including Fundy’s; it is not part of the Fundy app.

Phone permissions

  • Camera: to scan QR codes for addresses and payments.
  • Biometrics: to unlock your keys. Your fingerprint or face data stays with Android; Fundy never sees it.
  • Location, Bluetooth, nearby Wi-Fi and local network: declared for features that find nearby devices for payments. Your location is not sent to us.
  • NFC: for unlocking a hardware security key, if you use one.
  • Internet: to reach the blockchain services listed above.

You can turn any of these off in Android’s settings. Features that need them won’t work, but the rest of the wallet will.

Children

Fundy isn’t directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone. Since the app doesn’t send personal information to us, we have none to hold.

Your choices and contact

Because we don’t collect personal information through Fundy, there’s nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. You can remove all wallet data from your phone by uninstalling the app. Make sure you have your recovery phrase first.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
